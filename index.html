TV Remote – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

TV Remote ("the app") is a remote control for Samsung Smart TVs, developed by Sandeep Jatav. This policy explains what information the app handles.

No account, no personal data collection

The app does not require an account and does not collect, sell or share personal information. The developer does not run any server that receives data from the app. The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your phone

To reconnect to your TV, the app stores the following only on your device: your TV's local IP address, name and MAC address (used to turn the TV on over Wi-Fi), the pairing token your TV issues when you press "Allow", and a random identifier used for YouTube casting. This data never leaves your phone except as described below and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Communication with your TV

Remote commands, typed text and app launches are sent directly from your phone to your TV over your local Wi-Fi network.

YouTube

When you search for or play a YouTube video on your TV, the app sends your search text and the video ID to YouTube (Google) to fetch results and start playback, and loads video thumbnails from YouTube. These requests are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Notifications: used to show a notification that lets you type text to the TV. Internet / local network: used to talk to your TV and to YouTube.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
